About Mary's Marine Canvas

Custom work.
Personal service.

Mary's Marine Canvas has provided boat canvas and upholstery services since 2021.

The business focuses on custom marine work made around the boat itself. That includes protective canvas, enclosures, upholstery, repairs, and other custom projects where fit and function matter.

The Business

Built around
the project.

Marine canvas and upholstery rarely work well as one-size-fits-all products.

Boats have different layouts, hardware, seating, access points, and uses. Mary's Marine Canvas approaches each job as its own project so the finished work can fit the boat properly and serve the purpose it was made for.

01
Custom Fit

Work is made around the actual dimensions and layout of the boat.

02
Practical Use

Access, coverage, visibility, comfort, and everyday use all matter.

03
Marine Materials

Materials are selected with marine conditions and regular outdoor use in mind.

04
Finished Details

Fit, seams, hardware, alignment, and finishing touches affect the final result.

Why Custom

Made for the boat
in front of us.

A better fit can make the finished project easier to use, cleaner in appearance, and more practical on the water.

Whether the job is a cover, enclosure, cushion, upholstery piece, or repair, custom work allows the finished result to be built around the specific boat instead of forcing the boat to fit a generic product.

The Process

Straightforward
from start to finish.

01

02

03

04

Fit
