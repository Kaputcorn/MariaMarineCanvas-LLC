Our Services

Custom marine work

From protective canvas and weather enclosures to custom marine upholstery, Mary's Marine Canvas creates work around the shape, use, and needs of each individual boat.

01 Boat Canvas

Protection made to fit the boat.

Custom canvas can help protect important areas of your boat from weather, sun, dirt, and everyday exposure. Each piece is made around the boat rather than relying on a generic fit.

  • Biminis
  • Cockpit Covers
  • Bridge Covers
  • Sail Covers
  • Navy Tops

02 Enclosures

More protection. Clear visibility.

Marine enclosures help make the boat more comfortable and usable when conditions change. The goal is to provide protection while maintaining visibility, access, and a clean fit.

  • Full Enclosures
  • Isinglass
  • Clear Panels
  • Dodgers
  • Panel Replacement

03 Marine Upholstery

Refresh the places you actually use.

Marine seating and upholstery should be comfortable, durable, and appropriate for regular use on the water. Custom work also allows the finished piece to fit the boat properly and maintain a consistent appearance.

  • Boat Seats
  • Cushions
  • Bolsters
  • Interior Upholstery
  • Exterior Upholstery

How It Works

From the first conversation
to the finished fit.

01

Consult

Tell us about your boat, what you need, and what you want the project to accomplish.

02

Measure

Measurements or patterns are taken from the actual boat so the work can be made for the correct fit.

03

Fabricate

The project is constructed using materials suited for marine use with attention to fit and finish.

04

Install

The completed work is fitted to the boat and checked before the project is finished.
